The public demo at [try.towbar.dev](https://try.towbar.dev) shows the current
Towbar dashboard with example repositories, Services, Datastores, servers, and
deployment history. Choose **Start demo** to open a ten-minute demo. You do
not need an account.

## Things to try

1. Open **Services**, choose **Example Website**, and deploy it. Watch a
   simulated deployment progress from queued to succeeded.
2. Open a server and change its name. The change is visible only in your
   demo.
3. Explore deployment logs, monitoring, backup status, and service settings.
4. Pause automatic deployments for an example service, then use **Reset demo**
   to return to the original data.

All operations are simulated. The demo cannot deploy to a real server, connect
an integration, send a notification, or open a terminal. Credentials and team
changes are disabled. Do not enter personal information or secrets.

## Your session

The notice above the dashboard shows the time remaining. After ten minutes,
your demo ends and its changes reset. Start another demo to keep
exploring. **Reset demo** starts fresh immediately; **End demo** and sign-out
end the session.

Tabs in the same browser share a demo. Other browsers and visitors have
separate demos, including people using the same internet connection.
The demo can also reset during maintenance. If the demo is busy or asks you to wait,
try again in a few minutes.

To connect real repositories and servers,
[install your own Towbar instance](/docs/self-hosting/installation).
