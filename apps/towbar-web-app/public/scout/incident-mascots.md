# Overview illustration prompt

Create a minimal soft 3D dashboard illustration on an actual transparent PNG
background with alpha. Do not draw a checkerboard, floor, or backdrop. Keep the
objects opaque.

Compose for the bottom-right corner of a widget: objects extend beyond the bottom
and right canvas boundaries and are visibly cut off by those edges. Leave
transparent space above and to the left, without a transparent gutter along the
lower-right edge. Keep Scout's face fully visible. Use a square canvas.

Use consistent simple rounded shapes, broad shallow vertical container grooves,
smooth matte plastic surfaces, and soft neutral lighting. Avoid small hardware,
bolts, fine corrugation, outlines, realistic metal or fur texture, cloudy patches,
and mottling. Keep the subjects recognizable at small widget sizes.

Use darker neutral charcoal gray, around #454545 to #505050 on the main lit faces,
clearly lighter than black. No blue or cyan tint. Use warm yellow for the emblems,
ship accents, and Scout. Match container shapes and materials across the set.

Generate one image for each subject:

- **Apps:** Two overlapping gray containers, with a yellow Docker whale emblem on
  the front container.
- **Resources:** The same container composition, with a yellow database cylinder
  emblem replacing the Docker emblem.
- **Servers:** A simple gray cargo ship facing outward from the bottom-right
  corner, carrying gray containers. Use a single yellow trim stripe and yellow
  cabin windows. No mast, rigging, or tiny details.
- **No active incidents:** Happy yellow Scout beside gray containers. No smoke or
  red cargo.
- **Active incidents:** Worried yellow Scout with wings near its cheeks beside
  gray containers and one red container emitting soft gray smoke puffs. Keep smoke
  clear of Scout's face.
