# Integration provider logos

Bundled locally so the Integrations UI does not contact an external image host.

- `github.svg` and `slack.svg`: [Devicon v2.17.0](https://github.com/devicons/devicon/tree/v2.17.0/icons), MIT.
- `discord.svg` and `telegram.svg`: [Iconify's Logos collection](https://github.com/iconify/icon-sets/blob/ad784891fdb45ab99c31908c58c759333acb6571/json/logos.json) (Gil Barbara's SVG Logos), CC0-1.0. The original `discord-icon` and `telegram` artwork retains its view box and colors.
- OCI registries and Infisical reuse `/resource-logos/docker.webp` and `/resource-logos/infisical.webp`; their sources and licenses are recorded in the [resource logo catalog](../resource-logos/README.md).
- `doppler.ico`: Doppler's published dashboard favicon, copied unchanged from `https://dashboard.doppler.com/favicon.ico` on 2026-09-20.

These marks identify the configured integration providers. Names and artwork belong to their respective owners and do not imply endorsement. Collection licenses do not replace brand guidelines. Theme-specific variants follow the application's light or dark theme without adding a background tile.
