# Integration provider logos

Bundled locally so the Integrations UI does not contact an external image host.

- `github.svg` and `slack.svg`: [Devicon v2.17.0](https://github.com/devicons/devicon/tree/v2.17.0/icons), MIT.
- `discord.svg` and `telegram.svg`: [Iconify's Logos collection](https://github.com/iconify/icon-sets/blob/ad784891fdb45ab99c31908c58c759333acb6571/json/logos.json) (Gil Barbara's SVG Logos), CC0-1.0. The original `discord-icon` and `telegram` artwork retains its view box and colors.
- `newrelic.svg` and `newrelic-dark.svg`: green and white emblems from [New Relic's media assets](https://newrelic.com/about/media-assets), copied unchanged on 2026-09-17.
- `datadog.svg` and `datadog-dark.svg`: RGB and white SVG icons from [Datadog's press kit](https://www.datadoghq.com/about/resources/) (`Logo_Assets.zip`, `DD Vector Logos/Icon/SVG`), copied unchanged on 2026-09-17.
- `otlp.svg` and `otlp-dark.svg`: color and white icons from [OpenTelemetry's CNCF artwork](https://github.com/cncf/artwork/tree/main/projects/opentelemetry/icon), copied unchanged on 2026-09-17.
- `axiom.ico`: [Axiom's published favicon](https://axiom.co/favicon.ico), copied unchanged on 2026-09-17.
- `betterstack.png`: [Better Stack's published favicon](https://betterstack.com/assets/favicon-64296699.png), copied unchanged on 2026-09-17.
- OCI registries and Infisical reuse `/resource-logos/docker.webp` and `/resource-logos/infisical.webp`; their sources and licenses are recorded in the [resource logo catalog](../resource-logos/README.md).
- `doppler.ico`: Doppler's published dashboard favicon, copied unchanged from `https://dashboard.doppler.com/favicon.ico` on 2026-09-20.
- `otlp.svg` and `loki.svg`: the same pinned Iconify Logos collection above, using its `opentelemetry-icon` and `grafana` artwork unchanged.

These marks identify the configured integration providers. Names and artwork belong to their respective owners and do not imply endorsement. Collection licenses do not replace brand guidelines. Theme-specific variants follow the application's light or dark theme without adding a background tile. Axiom retains its black background.
